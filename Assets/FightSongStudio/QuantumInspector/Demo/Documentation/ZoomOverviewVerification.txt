Semantic zoom verification - 13 October 2026

Unity 6000.3.7f1; Windows; Intel Core i9-12900; NVIDIA RTX 3070;
65,222 MB system memory. UI Toolkit, Cosmic Nebula, Balanced quality.

Implemented: cards fade and contract into selectable round dots below their
real family/containing-folder header. Semantic zoom range 78%-38%; Full settles
in 180 ms; Reduced/Off settle immediately. Zoom floor remains 0.5%.
Counts, identity, saved graph positions, expansion and pins are preserved.
Selected dots have rings, pins have lower bars, disabled dots are hollow and
missing components have crossed hollow dots. Context actions and double-click
focus remain available. Selection details retain the full component name.

Passed in the running Editor:
- Real Dense prefab: 48 nodes / 89 edges, 42 dots and 6 owner/family cards.
- Dense checked at 1180x650 and 860x600 window sizes.
- Synthetic stress: 2,008 nodes / 4,499 edges, all 2,000 instance dots beneath
  8 headers at 860x600; graph viewport approximately 860x409 logical pixels.
- Every displayed dot hit-tests to its exact instance, including duplicates.
- Complete family footprints fit without overlap in those fixtures.
- Selected pinned member remains a dot; double-click restores its full card.
- Direct dot dragging follows the pointer; panning moves packed dots.
- Deterministic distinct dot slots, interrupted/reversed zoom, Reduced/Off
  settlement, deliberate collapse hiding, original positions/pins preserved.
- 1,000 idle ticks produced zero view updates after settlement.
- Unity compilation: zero errors/warnings. No new Console errors/warnings.

Stress overview pan CPU binding: 30 samples, average 5.458 ms, maximum 5.991 ms.
Stress inspection pan: average 1.843 ms, maximum 2.486 ms.
Maximum sampled art callback across the final stress session: 18.302 ms;
dot mesh submission maximum 4.012 ms. These cover main-thread preparation and
submission, not native tessellation, GPU time or end-to-end frame time. No FPS
or zero-allocation claim: the allocation counter failed its control check.

Small-window overflow found during verification was fixed by reserving whole
family footprints. Ordinary filled dots use a smaller mesh than outlined states.

Verification entry points: QuantumGraphOverviewVerification.Run(); for the
live harness, QuantumGraphPresentationVerification.Begin(42 or 2000), allow
an Editor frame, InspectView(window.Graph), then Complete() to restore state
and close the temporary window. InspectView requires the harness's Off mode.

Limitations: screenshot-based art review remains unverified; the available
Pipeline screenshot API captures Scene/Game views, not this EditorWindow.
Extremely long headers or more families than a dock can physically contain
use the existing overflow placement; pan, search and explicit focus remain
available. These checks do not certify every project, DPI or graph size.
