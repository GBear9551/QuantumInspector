QUANTUM INSPECTOR - QUICK START - 1.0.0
Developed by Gary Lougheed & Astra

Tested environment: Unity 6000.3.7f1, Windows.
Demo dependency: Universal RP 17.3.0 (use a compatible URP project).
The core Editor tool does not require Unity CLI, Pipeline or Asset Store Tools.

1. Import the tool scripts, settings and styles together and let Unity compile.
2. Choose Tools > Quantum Inspector > Open.
3. Select a GameObject in the Hierarchy; choose Wheel or Graph.
4. Scroll the wheel to rotate. In the graph, drag to pan and scroll to zoom.
5. Show Info toggles component details and history. Bookmarks stays in the header.
6. Use Parent, Roots, Back and Forward to navigate.
7. For the URP sample, save your work and choose Tools > Quantum Inspector > Demo > Open Demo Scene.
8. Search components in Quantum to filter Unity's Inspector, or use its new
   Components search field directly. Selecting a component scrolls it into view.
   See NativeInspectorSearch.txt for lock behavior and compatibility details.
9. Click folders in the Project window's left tree or items in its right pane
   to update Quantum. Clicking an already-selected asset works too. Unlock
   Quantum's Unity selection lock to resume following Project clicks.

Read QuantumInspector-UserGuide.pdf for settings, locks, editing, troubleshooting
and known limitations. Use Tools > Quantum Inspector > Layouts > Workspace Setup
for the recommended three-column layout. Arrange native docks manually and save
the result through Unity's Layouts menu; existing docks are preserved.
